## 📜 Full Store Description (Google Play Store)

Welcome to **3D Wizard Chess**, the ultimate immersive 3D chess experience set within a magnificent Gothic Cathedral!

Experience classic chess transformed with real-time 3D graphics, physical stone piece shattering animations, dynamic lighting, and tactile Android vibration haptics on every move.

### 🌟 Key Features:
- 🏛️ **Immersive 3D Cathedral:** Play upon an obsidian altar surrounded by towering Gothic arches, rose window sunlight, and warm torchlight.
- ♟️ **Sculpted 3D Pieces:** Masterfully sculpted chess pieces that duel, strike, and shatter on capture.
- 🧠 **Single Player AI & Local Altar:** Practice against intelligent AI or play face-to-face on a single device.
- 🌐 **Online Multiplayer:** Challenge friends or online opponents anywhere with instant room codes.
- 🛒 **Sanctum Armory & Cosmetics:** Customize your 3D chessboard with:
  - **3D Scenes:** Gothic Cathedral, Volcanic Forge, Celestial Void.
  - **Piece Sets:** Sculpted Stone, 24K Gold & Obsidian, Stained Glass.
  - **Board Themes:** Classic Ashlar, Carrara Marble, Lava Rune Stone.
- 🏆 **Tournaments & Leaderboards:** Compete in live arena tournaments to climb global leaderboards and earn exclusive cosmetic rewards!
- 📳 **Android Haptic Feedback:** Feel the weight and vibration pulse of every move and king fall.
