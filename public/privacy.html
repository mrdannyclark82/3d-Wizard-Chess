Privacy Policy for 3D Wizard Chess

Effective Date: October 17, 2025

Sanctum Gaming ("we", "us", or "our") built 3D Wizard Chess (Package: com.sanctum.wizardchess) as a Freemium app. This SERVICE is provided by Sanctum Gaming at no cost for base gameplay and is intended for use as is.

1. Information Collection and Use

3D Wizard Chess does not collect, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII). All gameplay data, single-player AI states, and unlocked cosmetic themes (scenes, piece sets, and board materials) are saved locally on your device's internal storage.

2. In-App Purchases & Billing

Optional cosmetic items (3D scenes, piece sets, and board themes) are sold via Google Play In-App Billing. All payment transactions, credit card handling, and financial processing are handled securely and directly by Google Play Store. We do not receive or store your financial or payment details.

3. Network & Online Multiplayer

When participating in online multiplayer matchmaker rooms, temporary room connection tokens are used solely to establish peer-to-peer or server-mediated move transmission between players. No personal identifiers or profile data are associated with these game rooms.

4. Device Permissions

3D Wizard Chess requests the following standard Android device permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Used for online multiplayer room matchmaking.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — Used to trigger tactile haptic vibration pulses on piece captures and checkmate.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — Used for processing optional Google Play Store cosmetic purchases.

5. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: support@sanctumwizardchess.com.
