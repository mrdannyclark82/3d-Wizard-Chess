# Privacy Policy for 3D Wizard Chess

**Effective Date:** October 17, 2025  
**Developer:** Sanctum Gaming (`com.sanctum.wizardchess`)

Sanctum Gaming built **3D Wizard Chess** as a Freemium app. This service is provided at no cost for base gameplay and is intended for use as is.

---

### 1. Information Collection & Use
3D Wizard Chess does **not** collect, track, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). All game progress, single-player AI states, and unlocked cosmetics are stored locally on your device.

---

### 2. In-App Purchases & Billing
Optional cosmetic purchases (3D scenes, piece sets, and board themes) are processed securely through **Google Play Billing**. Sanctum Gaming does not collect or store credit card numbers, billing addresses, or financial data.

---

### 3. Device Permissions
- `INTERNET`: Required for online multiplayer room matchmaking.
- `VIBRATE`: Enables tactile Android vibration feedback on piece strikes and checkmate.
- `BILLING`: Enables optional Google Play Store cosmetic purchases.

---

### 4. Children's Privacy
3D Wizard Chess does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 and complies with COPPA and GDPR requirements.
